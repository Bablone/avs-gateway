# Research Plan: v0.3.6 ASR-1 + Agent Identity Validation

## Research Questions

1. **Receipt/audit standards landscape**: What standards exist for machine-actionable audit receipts? W3C Verifiable Credentials, IETF, cloud-native audit formats?
2. **Agent identity standards**: What exists for agent identity? SPIFFE/SPIRE, OAuth for software (RFC 7523), workload identity, mTLS?
3. **Competitive reality check**: Who else is working on agent receipts, agent identity, or action attestation? What are they doing?
4. **License analysis**: BSL 1.1 vs Apache 2.0 — what's the HashiCorp precedent, community reaction, adoption impact?
5. **Technical feasibility**: Tool integrity hashing in Python — real challenges with decorators, closures, dynamic imports. What actually works?
6. **OWASP Agentic AI 2026**: Current status, what's being standardized, where can AVS plug in?
